Request a GPS Collar for Your Dog

A GPS collar is one of the simplest ways to keep a newly rescued dog safe in those first frightened weeks — when a slipped leash or a gap in the fence can mean the difference between a scare and a tragedy. If you’ve adopted, are fostering, or are a rescue placing a dog from a research laboratory, breeding facility, or other traumatic setting, you can request a donated GPS collar through Omelette’s Promise.

Tell us a little about your dog using the form below and we’ll be in touch. There’s no cost to you — this is exactly what Omelette’s Promise exists to do.

Request a GPS Collar ↗

Please remember: a GPS collar only protects a dog once it’s activated and connected to a phone app. We’ll help you get it set up before your dog ever wears it out the door.
